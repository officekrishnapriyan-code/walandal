01 Flagship

Aqua Insight.

Ensuring trust in every drop.

Rural overhead tanks are chlorinated by hand, so water is often under- or over-dosed and nobody sees it until people fall ill. Aqua Insight puts a solar-powered sensor kit on every tank, doses chlorine automatically, and gives everyone one live picture of water they can trust.

Real-time water quality

Free chlorine, pH, TDS, turbidity and tank level, streamed from every tank and checked against drinking-water limits as it arrives.

Closed-loop chlorine dosing

When residual chlorine drops, the cloud sends a dose command straight back to the tank. Operators can switch to manual and dose on demand.

Predictive alerts

Trend analysis warns before a limit is crossed, so a low-chlorine tank gets attention hours before it becomes unsafe.

One score: Water Quality Index

A 0–100 score for every tank, village and district that anyone can read at a glance, from a resident to a district officer.

Compliance, ready to share

IS 10500 compliance by village, incidents and response times, exported as a PDF report for audits and review meetings.

Built for everyone on the chain

Owners, field operators and district authorities each see what they need, and residents get a plain-language “Is the water safe?” card.
