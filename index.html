01 / FLAGSHIP

Aqua Insight.

Ensuring trust in every drop.

Millions of people in rural India drink from overhead tanks and borewells, where manual chlorination either under-doses or over-doses and nobody sees the problem until people fall ill. Aqua Insight puts a solar-powered sensor kit on every tank, doses chlorine automatically, and gives owners, operators and authorities one live picture of water they can trust.

Real-time water quality

Free chlorine, pH, TDS, turbidity and tank level, streamed from every tank and checked against drinking-water limits as it arrives.

Closed-loop chlorine dosing

When residual chlorine drops, the cloud sends a dose command straight back to the tank. Operators can switch to manual and dose on demand.

Predictive alerts

Trend analysis warns before a limit is crossed, so a low-chlorine tank gets attention hours before it becomes unsafe.

One score: Water Quality Index

A 0–100 score for every tank, village and district that anyone can read at a glance, from a resident to a district officer.

Compliance, ready to share

IS 10500 compliance by village, incidents and response times, exported as a PDF report for audits and review meetings.

Built for everyone on the chain

Owners, field operators and district authorities each see what they need, and residents get a plain-language “Is the water safe?” card.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

From sensor to decision, in one loop.

Every reading travels the same hardened path: measured at the tank, secured in flight, checked in the cloud, and acted on, either by people through the app or by the tank itself through the doser.

  1. 01

    Sense

    Solar ESP32 kit on the tank: chlorine, pH, TDS, turbidity, level, and a chlorine doser.

  2. 02

    Connect

    MQTT over TLS into AWS IoT Core, with a certificate for each device.

  3. 03

    Buffer

    IoT rules route every message into a durable queue, so no reading is lost.

  4. 04

    Process

    Containers validate, store and evaluate each reading against safe limits.

  5. 05

    Remember

    A time-series database keeps every tank's history for trends and audits.

  6. 06

    Act

    Alerts, the mobile app, and reports, plus a dose command back to the tank.
