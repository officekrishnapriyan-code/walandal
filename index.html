02 / HOW IT WORKS

From sensor to decision, in one loop.

Measured at the tank, secured in flight, checked in the cloud, then acted on by people through the app or by the tank itself through the doser.

  1. 01

    Sense

    Solar ESP32 kit with five sensors and a chlorine doser.

  2. 02

    Connect

    MQTT over TLS to AWS IoT Core, one certificate per device.

  3. 03

    Buffer

    A durable queue, so no reading is lost.

  4. 04

    Process

    Containers validate each reading against safe limits.

  5. 05

    Remember

    Time-series history for trends and audits.

  6. 06

    Act

    Alerts, app and reports, plus a dose back to the tank.
