02 / HOW IT WORKS

From sensor to decision, in one loop.

Measured at the edge, secured in flight, processed in the cloud, then acted on by people through the dashboards or automatically by the devices themselves.

  1. 01

    Sense

    Solar ESP32 nodes with multi-parameter sensors and actuators.

  2. 02

    Connect

    MQTT over TLS to AWS IoT Core, one certificate per device.

  3. 03

    Buffer

    A durable queue, so no reading is lost.

  4. 04

    Process

    Containers validate each reading against safe limits.

  5. 05

    Remember

    Time-series history for trends and audits.

  6. 06

    Act

    Alerts, dashboards and reports, plus commands back to the field.

Telemetry    Closed-loop control command
